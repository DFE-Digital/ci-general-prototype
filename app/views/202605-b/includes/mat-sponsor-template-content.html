Rationale

Rationale for the project

Greenhill Primary School is a community primary school located in Bury, accepting children between the ages of 4 and 11. The school has 205 pupils on roll, a school capacity of 210 (98% full), and has no recorded SEN or resourced provisions. In terms of the school's pupil population for 2023/24, 3.3% of pupils have a Special Education Needs (SEN) Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP), slightly above the national figure of 3.0%.
